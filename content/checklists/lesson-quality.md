## When to use

Use this checklist after completing a lesson and before submitting it for review.

## Content

- [ ] Learning objective is clear
- [ ] Content matches the learning objective
- [ ] Information is accurate
- [ ] Language is appropriate for the grade level

## Activity

- [ ] Activity instructions are clear
- [ ] Materials are listed
- [ ] Steps are in the correct order
- [ ] Activity is practical and testable

## Story

- [ ] Story creates curiosity
- [ ] Characters are consistent
- [ ] Story connects naturally to the activity

## Final Review

- [ ] Spelling and grammar checked
- [ ] Images and text are aligned
