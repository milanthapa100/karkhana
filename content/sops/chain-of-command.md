## Description

This SOP defines the chain of command within the DPK unit: who reports to whom, who makes which decisions, and where questions or problems should be escalated. It exists so every member knows their reporting path and can get answers without confusion.

## Steps

1. **Handle it at your level first.** If you can resolve a question or issue yourself, do so before escalating.
2. **Go to your direct point of contact.** If you need input, approval, or help, go to the person one step above you in the chain.
3. **Escalate one level at a time.** If the issue is not resolved, raise it to the next level. Do not skip levels except in emergencies.
4. **Keep the relevant people informed.** Let the right people know about significant decisions or unresolved issues so nothing is missed.

## Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Unit Member | Owns their own tasks; reports to the point of contact above them |
| Unit Lead / Point of Contact | Guides unit members; makes routine decisions and answers questions |
| Department Heads | Make higher-level decisions and resolve issues escalated from the units |
| Org Leadership | Make strategic decisions; final authority on major matters |
